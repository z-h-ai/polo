# Upload an App to Polo

## Current availability (read this first)

**This version of the Polo client has not connected app upload yet.** The trusted
upload tool is not available in this runtime, so you cannot upload anything today.

- Say clearly that uploading is not available in the current version yet.
- Do NOT pretend an upload succeeded, and do NOT fabricate an API, endpoint or manifest.
- Do NOT perform any network side effect: no upload requests, no `curl`, no calls to
  retired or admin-only endpoints, and never borrow admin/management credentials.
- Do NOT read tokens, cookies or credential storage to attempt an upload yourself.
- You may still help with the local material checklist below so the app is ready
  when upload ships.

## When to use this Skill

The member asks to upload, submit, publish or share an app they built with Polo,
or asks what happens after an app is submitted.

## Who may upload

- An active member of an enterprise may submit an app to that enterprise.
- A user with valid creator qualification may submit an app to the platform.
- Inactive, revoked or read-only membership means no upload: say so and stop.
- The target enterprise or catalog must be named by the member. Never pick a
  target on their behalf.

## What may be uploaded

- Only the app the member has already finished and explicitly pointed at: a
  local directory or package they named. Never upload unrelated files, and never
  re-request approval for a target and file set the member has already
  authorized in this conversation.
- The platform manifest is generated by the platform during upload. Never write,
  edit or fake a manifest to look uploadable, and never change the app identity
  the member declared.

## Local material checklist (usable today)

- App directory contains a runnable, self-contained build the member owns.
- Name, version and short description are decided by the member.
- Screenshots or demo assets the member wants to attach are collected locally.
- No secrets, tokens or private credentials are inside the material.

## Once upload tooling is connected (future contract)

Treat these as the state rules to apply then; none of them are available now.

- Upload and safety checks are platform steps; enterprise human review is a
  separate step and passing safety checks never implies approval by the
  enterprise, and vice versa.
- Report exactly one real state, never a blended one: **uploaded** (received by
  the platform), **pending review** (waiting for enterprise review),
  **published** (visible in the catalog), **shared** (distributed to members).
- If a network result is unknown (timeout, interrupted turn), query the original
  request by its idempotent identity first; never blindly upload again.
